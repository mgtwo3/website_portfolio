One product. Three creative directions.

Designed a workflow prototype that combines a product image, a creative brief, and target locations to develop localized advertising concepts. The setup uses Gemini to plan scenes for 5–10-second commercials, with instructions to preserve product appearance and create authentic settings without stereotypes.
